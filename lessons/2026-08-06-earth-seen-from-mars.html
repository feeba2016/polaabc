Earth Seen From Mars

NASA's rover on Mars caught Earth hiding behind a tiny moon. Read along — tap any highlighted word to see its meaning. 🔊 play a segment to hear it.

Segment 1 · Hook

Imagine standing on the surface of Mars and looking up at the sky. You would see our distant home, Earth — but only as a tiny dot of light. This week, a robot on Mars captured a very special view of our planet.

Segment 2 · Story

NASA's Perseverance rover used its camera to film Earth in the Martian sky. On July 2, our planet slid behind Phobos, Mars' little potato-shaped moon. It was the first time we have ever seen Earth hide behind another world.

Segment 3 · Why it matters

From Mars, Earth is just one pixel of light — about 314 million kilometers away. This reminds us how small and special our planet really is. It also shows how clever scientists must be to catch such a rare moment.

Segment 4 · Sign-off

Next time you look at the night sky, remember this little fact. From Mars, you would be nothing but a tiny twinkle. This is Pola, signing off from POLA NEWS. See you next time!

句型练习 Sentence Patterns

5 useful patterns taken from the report. Try writing your own sentence!

1. Imagine + verb-ing …
"Imagine standing on the surface of Mars and looking up at the sky."
用法：用 Imagine + 动名词 邀请对方想象一个场景。
✏️ Your turn: Imagine _______________________________ .
2. From [place], [thing] is just …
"From Mars, Earth is just one pixel of light."
用法：用 From + 地点 说明从某处看某物是什么样子。
✏️ Your turn: From the top of the hill, the town is just ________________ .
3. It was the first time we have ever …
"It was the first time we have ever seen Earth hide behind another world."
用法：It was the first time + 现在完成时 表示"这是我们有史以来第一次……"。
✏️ Your turn: It was the first time I have ever ________________ .
4. This reminds us how + adjective …
"This reminds us how small and special our planet really is."
用法：This reminds us how + 形容词 表达某事让我们想起某种感受或事实。
✏️ Your turn: This reminds us how ________________ our planet is.
5. Next time you + verb …, remember that …
"Next time you look at the night sky, remember this little fact."
用法：用 Next time you … 引出一条建议或提醒。
✏️ Your turn: Next time you ____________, remember to ________________ .

语法点 Grammar

5 grammar points with a quick practice each.

① Past Simple vs. Present Perfect
"Perseverance captured the view." (a finished action)   vs   "It was the first time we have ever seen it." (up to now)
过去简单时讲"已经做完的事"；"It was the first time + 现在完成时"讲"直到现在为止第一次"。
✏️ Fill: This is the first time I          (see) snow.
② Prepositions of place: behind / from / on
"Earth hid behind Phobos."   "seen from Mars."   "standing on the surface."
behind = 在…后面；from = 从…（看）；on = 在…表面。
✏️ Fill: The cat is hiding          the sofa.
③ Comparatives: smaller / more special
"This reminds us how small and special our planet is."   (compare with: the Moon looks bigger from Earth)
单音节词 + -er（small → smaller）；多音节词用 more（more special）。
✏️ Fill: Mars is          (small) than Earth.
④ "It was the first time …" + Present Perfect
"It was the first time we have ever seen Earth hide behind another world."
在 "the first time" 之后习惯用现在完成时（have/has + 过去分词）。
✏️ Fill: It was the first time he          (ride) a horse.
⑤ Modal verb: must (for necessity)
"…how clever scientists must be to catch such a rare moment."
must + 动词原形 表示"一定／必须"，这里表示推断"科学家一定很聪明"。
✏️ Fill: To fly to Mars, we          build a very strong rocket.

中文知识扩展 Learn More

🌟 一、"暗弱蓝点"——从远方看地球

1990 年，NASA 的旅行者 1 号（Voyager 1）飞船飞到太阳系边缘时，回过头给地球拍了一张照片。在画面里，地球只是一个暗淡的蓝色小点。天文学家卡尔·萨根（Carl Sagan）由此写下名言："再看那个点，那就是这里，那就是家，那就是我们。" 这次毅力号从火星拍到地球"躲"到火卫一后面，正是这种"从远方看家乡"视角的又一次动人延续。

🛰️ 二、认识火卫一 Phobos（福博斯）

  • 形状：像个凹凸不平的"土豆"，最宽处约 27 公里。
  • 轨道：离火星表面仅约 7,800 公里，是太阳系中离母行星最近的卫星之一；每天横穿火星天空约 3 次。
  • 潮汐锁定：和月球一样，它总是用同一面朝向火星。
  • 未来：因为离火星太近，科学家估计几千万年后它可能会撞上火星或碎成环。

📏 三、尺度与视角：为什么地球只是一个像素？

拍照片时，地球距离火星约 3.14 亿公里，所以被压缩成一个针尖大小的光点。而近在咫尺的火卫一反而显得有地球上看到的月球约三分之一宽。这告诉我们：同一个物体，距离不同，看起来差别巨大。下次在操场远处看同学，你也能体会到这种"透视"。

🔭 四、为什么这次拍摄很难？

火卫一每天横穿天空 3 次，地球也能连续好几个月可见，但要让一个天体正好躲到另一个正后方（天文学叫"掩食 / occultation"），既需要提前精打细算，也需要一点点运气。毅力号用桅杆相机 Mastcam-Z，在火星傍晚（日落后约 40 分钟）连拍多张，再由科学家把图像拼合，才得到这组画面。

🤖 五、毅力号在做什么？

毅力号（Perseverance）2021 年登陆火星的杰泽罗陨石坑（Jezero Crater），那里曾是古老的湖泊三角洲。它的主要任务是在火星岩石中寻找古代微生物生命的迹象，并收集样本，等待未来的飞船取回地球。这次"拍地球"是它在科学工作之余，顺手送给我们的一份宇宙浪漫。

💡 六、小启发

从火星看，我们的家园只是一个闪烁的小点——没有国界、没有争吵，只有一颗脆弱又珍贵的蓝色星球。这提醒我们：地球虽小，却是我们唯一的家，值得好好保护。
