A Truth Universally Acknowledged

简·奥斯汀《傲慢与偏见》开篇精读 ｜ 全文阅读（点高亮词看中文释义，点 ▶ 听朗读）

1
Paragraph 1 · The famous opening

It is a truth universally acknowledged, that a single man in possession of a good fortune, must be in want of a wife. However little known the feelings or views of such a man may be on his first entering a neighbourhood, this truth is so well fixed in the minds of the surrounding families, that he is considered the rightful property of some one or other of their daughters.

2
Paragraph 2 · The dialogue

"My dear Mr. Bennet," said his lady to him one day, "have you heard that Netherfield Park is let at last?" Mr. Bennet replied that he had not. "But it is," returned she; "for Mrs. Long has just been here, and she told me all about it." Mr. Bennet made no answer. "Do you not want to know who has taken it?" cried his wife impatiently. "You want to tell me, and I have no objection to hearing it." This was invitation enough.

3
Paragraph 3 · The characters

Mr. Bennet was so odd a mixture of quick parts, sarcastic humour, reserve, and caprice, that the experience of three-and-twenty years had been insufficient to make his wife understand his character. She was a woman of mean understanding, little information, and uncertain temper. The business of her life was to get her daughters married; its solace was visiting and news.

4
Paragraph 4 · Close reading

With these lines, Austen announces her subject: marriage as social machinery. The sentence wears the costume of a solemn maxim, yet swerves into something comic. Read closely, the so-called truth is not about bachelors at all, but about the families who hunt them.

💡 句型练习 Sentence Patterns (B2 Advanced)

1. It is a truth universally acknowledged, that + clause.

形式主语 It + that 从句（外位结构 / 强调结构）。以"被普遍承认的真理"起笔，语气庄重，是奥斯汀用来制造反讽的招牌句式。
It is a truth universally acknowledged, that a single man in possession of a good fortune must be in want of a wife.
练习：用 "it is a truth universally acknowledged / hard work brings its own reward" → , that hard work brings its own reward.

2. However + adjective + subject + may + verb, …

让步状语从句倒装：However 提前到形容词前，主语与情态动词 may 倒装，相当于 "No matter how little known…"，语气正式、书面。
However little known the feelings of such a man may be, he is considered the rightful property of someone's daughter.
练习：用 "however difficult the task / he finished it" → , he finished it with a smile.

3. so + adjective + that + clause (passive).

so…that 结果状语从句，主句常用被动语态，表示"如此……以至于被……"。本句中 "so well fixed … that he is considered…" 即典型用法。
This truth is so well fixed in the minds of the families, that he is considered the rightful property of their daughters.
练习：用 "so widely loved / the book was translated into many languages" → The novel was , that it was translated into many languages.

4. Subject + be + a noun phrase of [A], [B], and [C].

用 of 短语 + 平行名词结构给人物下定义，形成排比，是文学描写与人物速写的经典句式。
She was a woman of mean understanding, little information, and uncertain temper.
练习：用 "he was a man of courage, wisdom, and kindness" → courage, wisdom, and kindness.

5. The experience of [time] had been insufficient to + infinitive.

过去完成时 + 不定式：表示"到过去某一刻为止，某段经历仍不足以……"，强调一种先于叙述时间、并持续到当时的状态。
The experience of three-and-twenty years had been insufficient to make his wife understand his character.
练习：用 "his words had been insufficient to calm her" → to calm her fears.

📐 语法点 Grammar

1. 形式主语 It + that 从句（Extraposition）

真正主语是后面的 that 从句，It 是形式主语，把长主语"后置"，使句子更平衡、更庄重。奥斯汀用 "It is a truth universally acknowledged, that…" 模仿格言口吻。
It is a truth universally acknowledged, that a single man … must be in want of a wife.

2. 让步状语从句倒装（Inverted concessive clause）

However / No matter how + 形容词/副词 置于句首时，主语与情态动词 may/might 形成倒装。比 "Although…" 更书面、更具文学性。
However little known the feelings of such a man may be, this truth is so well fixed in the minds of the families.

3. 被动语态（Passive voice）

"he is considered the rightful property" 把"被视作"的焦点放在单身汉身上，暗含"他其实没有主动权"的反讽——整个社会替他做了主。
…that he is considered the rightful property of some one or other of their daughters.

4. 介词短语 / 过去分词作定语（Post-modification）

"in possession of a good fortune"（介词短语作后置定语）、"a woman of mean understanding"（of 短语下定义），把修饰成分放在名词之后，结构紧凑、书面味浓。
a single man in possession of a good fortune / a woman of mean understanding, little information, and uncertain temper.

5. 过去完成时（Past perfect）

"had been insufficient" 表示在叙述的过去时间之前，某种状态(经历不足)已经持续存在，是回溯性叙述的常用时态。
The experience of three-and-twenty years had been insufficient to make his wife understand his character.

✅ 练习测验 Quiz

🌏 中文知识扩展

一句"真理"，半部婚姻浮世绘

"凡是有钱的单身汉，总想娶位太太，这已成为一条举世公认的真理。"——这大概是英语文学里最著名的开场白。它出自简·奥斯汀（Jane Austen，1775–1817）的《傲慢与偏见》（Pride and Prejudice，1813）。但这句话的妙处在于：它说的和它想说的，恰好相反。

反讽（Irony）：用哲学口吻说荒唐事

奥斯汀以"真理""普遍承认"这样一本正经的格言式语气开篇，读者以为要听什么宏大道理，结果下半句落到了"有钱单身汉想娶妻"这种家长里短的算计上。这种措辞与内容的错位，正是 verbal irony（言语反讽）。真正被"公认"的，不是单身汉想结婚，而是那些急于嫁女的家庭替他决定了需求——他不是猎人，而是被围猎的猎物。

为什么婚姻是"社会机器"？

要读懂这句反讽，得看当时的英国社会：

  • 限定继承（entail）：乡绅地产多由男性继承，班纳特家没有儿子，五个女儿无权继承父亲的家产。婚姻因此几乎是女性唯一的经济保障。
  • 婚姻市场：一段好亲事 = 财产的重新配置。所以"单身汉"会被默认为"属于某位女儿的合法财产（rightful property）"——这句话把人物当商品，本身就是辛辣讽刺。
  • 阶层与体面：摄政时代（Regency era, 1811–1820）的乡绅阶层，面子与联姻紧紧绑定，母亲们的焦虑由此而来。

叙事视角：全知却带反讽距离

这段用的是第三人称全知视角，却不是冷冰冰的旁白，而是带着一种"我比你还懂这社会"的俏皮口吻。学者认为这是后来 free indirect discourse（自由间接引语）的雏形——把人物心理和作者讽刺揉在一起，让读者既看故事、又看门道。

"universally acknowledged"的陷阱

谁定义了这条"普遍真理"？其实是既得利益者——急于嫁女的家庭、爱八卦的邻里。奥斯汀用一个词就点破：所谓常识，常常是某一群人替所有人下的结论。200 多年后，这句话仍被反复引用，正因为它精准戳中了"社会如何制造'理所当然'"的机制。

学习者的收获

细读（close reading）的乐趣，就在这种"句子说一套、意思另一套"的缝隙里。当你能指出"It is a truth universally acknowledged, that…" 其实是反讽，你就已经在进行真正的文学批评了。在 B2 写作中，学着用 However little known… may be、It is … that… 这类结构，能让你的英语多一分书面与思辨的质感。
