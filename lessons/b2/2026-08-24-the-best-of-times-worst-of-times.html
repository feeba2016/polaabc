It Was the Best of Times

查尔斯·狄更斯《双城记》开篇精读 ｜ 全文阅读（点高亮词看中文释义，点 ▶ 听朗读）

1
Paragraph 1 · The famous opening

It was the best of times, it was the worst of times, it was the age of wisdom, it was the age of foolishness, it was the epoch of belief, it was the epoch of incredulity, it was the season of Light, it was the season of Darkness, it was the spring of hope, it was the winter of despair, we had everything before us, we had nothing before us.

2
Paragraph 2 · What the line means

With these words, Charles Dickens opens his 1859 novel A Tale of Two Cities. On the surface, the sentence is a string of contradictions. Yet its power lies in this paradox: an era can be wonderful and dreadful at the same time. The paired opposites — wisdom and foolishness, Light and Darkness, hope and despair — mirror a world caught between progress and violence.

3
Paragraph 3 · The historical setting

The novel is set in London and Paris during the years around the French Revolution of 1789. Dickens wrote it in 1859, after the Industrial Revolution had already reshaped Britain. By holding two cities side by side, he implies that every age carries both light and darkness, and quietly warns his own society against the inequalities that had once fuelled France's upheaval.

4
Paragraph 4 · Close reading

Read aloud, the repeated phrase "it was" becomes a drumbeat, a rhythm that makes the sentence unforgettable. This device, anaphora, works with antithesis to show that history refuses simple labels. More than two centuries later, the line still resonates: prosperity and panic, hope and despair, existing simultaneously.

💡 句型练习 Sentence Patterns (B2 Advanced)

1. Anaphora: "It was [X], it was [Y], it was [Z]…"

首语重复：在同一位置的连续分句开头重复相同词语（It was），形成节奏感与朗诵的鼓点效果。狄更斯用此法让句子过耳难忘。
It was the best of times, it was the worst of times, it was the age of wisdom, it was the age of foolishness.
练习：用 "it was the season of joy / it was the season of sorrow" → , it was the season of sorrow.

2. Antithesis: parallel clauses with contrasting pairs.

对偶/对照：把意义相反的词（best/worst, wisdom/foolishness, Light/Darkness）放进结构平行的短语中，形成强烈反差，是修辞重点句型。
It was the age of wisdom, it was the age of foolishness; it was the season of Light, it was the season of Darkness.
练习：用 "the city of order / the city of chaos" → It was the city of order, .

3. Cleft sentence: It was [focus] that + clause.

割裂句（强调句）：用 "It was…that…" 把句子某一部分置于焦点，起到强调作用。文学与学术写作中常见。
It was the French Revolution that gave the novel its historical backdrop.
练习：用 "Dickens / wrote it in 1859" → It was that wrote it in 1859.

4. Past participle as adverbial: [V-pp], subject + verb…

过去分词作状语：句首用过去分词短语（如 Set in…）修饰主句，相当于一个省略的状语从句，书面味浓、结构紧凑。
Set in London and Paris, the novel contrasts two cities torn by inequality.
练习：用 "Published in 1859" → , the book became an instant classic.

5. Inverted subjunctive (third conditional): Had + subject + V-pp, …

虚拟语气倒装（与过去事实相反）：把 if 省略、将 had 提前至主语之前，语气更正式、书面。相当于 If Dickens had lived…, he would have…
Had Dickens lived a century earlier, he could not have written this historical novel.
练习：用 "had the Revolution been peaceful" → , the novel would have ended differently.

📐 语法点 Grammar

1. 首语重复与排比（Anaphora & Parallelism）

连续分句以 "It was" 起头，构成排比，既制造朗诵般的节奏，又让"好/坏""智/愚"等成对概念彼此撞击，强化张力。这是该句流传百年的关键。
It was the best of times, it was the worst of times, it was the age of wisdom…

2. 对偶 / 对照（Antithesis）

把语义相反的词置于平行结构中（Light/Darkness, hope/despair），让读者主动感受"同一时代何以兼有两种极端"。这是本段的核心修辞。
…it was the season of Light, it was the season of Darkness…

3. 割裂句（Cleft sentence: It was… that…）

"It was…that…" 把焦点成分突出，普通陈述句因此获得强调与庄重感。可用于写作中突出原因、人物或时间。
It was the French Revolution that gave the novel its backdrop.

4. 过去分词作状语（Past participle as adverbial）

"Set in London and Paris" 是省略的状语从句（= Because it was set…），置于句首修饰主句，使句子更简洁、书面化。
Set in London and Paris, the novel contrasts two cities.

5. 虚拟语气倒装（Inverted subjunctive）

"Had Dickens lived…, he would have…" 是与过去事实相反的虚拟条件句，省略 if 并将 had 提前。比 "If he had lived" 更正式、更具文学性。
Had Dickens lived a century earlier, he could not have written it.

🌏 中文知识扩展

一句话，撑起一个时代

"这是最好的时代，这是最坏的时代……"——英语文学里最著名的开场白之一，出自查尔斯·狄更斯（Charles Dickens，1812–1870）的《双城记》（A Tale of Two Cities，1859）。小说以法国大革命（1789–1799）为背景，故事在伦敦与巴黎之间展开，时间跨度约为 1775–1793 年。

悖论（Paradox）：矛盾如何同时为真

这句话的妙处，在于它不回避矛盾。狄更斯一口气列出成对的相反概念：best/worst（最好/最坏）、wisdom/foolishness（智慧/愚昧）、Light/Darkness（光明/黑暗）、hope/despair（希望/绝望）。他真正想说的是：一个时代可以"同时"既好又坏。启蒙运动带来了理性与对自由的向往（"最好的时代"），而同一股追求自由的力量，最终却引爆了血腥的恐怖统治（"最坏的时代"）。进步与暴力，从同一台社会机器里产出。

为什么是"双城"？

狄更斯把伦敦与巴黎并置，是在做一面镜子：

  • 法国大革命：1789 年攻陷巴士底狱，君主制被推翻，随后进入以断头台为标志的恐怖时期（Reign of Terror，1793–1794）。
  • 英国的工业革命：1760–1840 年代，技术飞速进步，却也伴随童工、贫民窟、每天十四小时以上的苦役与极端贫富分化。狄更斯生活在其中，亲眼目睹"国家极富、民众极贫"的悖论。
  • 他写法国，其实是在警告英国：若不解决不平等，同样的动荡迟早重演。

修辞的精密结构

这段文字是修辞教科书：

  • 首语重复（Anaphora）：连续以 "It was" 起头，像节拍器一样推进，让历史显得"不可阻挡"。
  • 对偶（Antithesis）：把相反词放进平行结构，制造记忆点与思辨张力。
  • 悖论式叙事：拒绝给时代贴上"好"或"坏"的单一标签，迫使读者接受复杂性。

跨越两个世纪的"头条"

狄更斯写于 1859 年，却像今天的新闻标题：繁荣与恐慌同屏，希望与绝望并存。学者认为，他用"喧闹的权威（noisy authorities）"讽刺当时舆论惯于用非黑即白的套话概括复杂事件——这一点放在任何时代都成立。

学习者的收获

在 B2 写作中，学着用 It was…that…（割裂句）突出重点，用 Had…, …would have…（虚拟倒装）表达反事实，再借 antithesis 与 anaphora 增添节奏与思辨质感，你的英语会多一分文学与论证的力量。
